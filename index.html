Flores Family Assistant

Flores Family Assistant is a private, personal-use tool run by one household. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

With each family member's own Google consent, it helps the household keep track of shared calendar events, family tasks, and school and household reminders.

It reads Google Calendar and a family Google Sheet, and reads email only with read-only access that each account owner authorizes themselves. It never sends, deletes, or changes email.
