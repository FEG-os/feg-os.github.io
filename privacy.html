Flores Family Assistant: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Who uses this app

Only members of the Flores household, each using their own Google account and their own consent. It is not available to the public.

Google data accessed

  • Google Calendar: to read family events and, where a family member asks, add or update events.
  • Google Sheets / Drive (single file): to read and write the household's own planning spreadsheet.
  • Gmail (read-only): to summarize messages such as school notices, schedules, and bills for the account owner. The app cannot send, delete, or modify email.

How data is used and stored

Data is processed on the household's own computer to produce reminders and summaries for the account owner. Access tokens are stored locally in the computer's secure keychain. Information from one person's account is not shared with other family members without that person's permission.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with third parties, except as needed to generate summaries for the account owner using the AI assistant tools the household already uses.

Limited Use

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Any account owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
